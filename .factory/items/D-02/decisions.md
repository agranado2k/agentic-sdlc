- 2026-10-23 **Q1 · High risk = not yet learned, or can't be learned from** (operator chose (b), my recommendation). High risk is either: (1) a change kind whose reviewing lenses have not met their precision floor over N landed outcomes; or (2) a change whose mistake would never show up as a countable outcome: a policy, guard or gate file; a shared-layer release; auth or secrets; or an unspecified behavior item on the confirm-list. Everything starts high risk and earns its way down.
- 2026-10-23 **A consumer with no factory gets the generic chain, kept in step with the ticket contract** (operator, confirmed in the D-08 session and relayed by it). New stamp fields, trace fields and rule changes reach the generic planning skills; new planning features land only in the factory. D-08 records the four boundary conditions the same way.
- 2026-10-23 **Q2 · A mistake = a revert, a later fix touching the PR's lines within 30 days, or a failed factory QA** (operator chose (c), my recommendation). A landed PR counts as a mistake when (1) it is reverted; or (2) within 30 days a later `fix:` commit or ticket naming the PR touches lines it introduced (git blame on the fix's diff); or (3) its factory ticket fails its QA gate. Each is recorded as a post-merge outcome event against the PR, and these outcomes are the labels the per-lens precision of Q1 is measured on. Reverts alone were too rare to learn from; QA adds the signal a spec-level miss leaves that no later fix would show.
- 2026-10-23 **Q3 · A change kind earns low risk on both misses and noise, thresholds in a policy file** (operator chose (c), my recommendation). A change kind is low risk when, over its last 20 landed PRs, fewer than 1 in 20 carried a mistake (Q2) the review missed, and at least 80% of its lens findings held up. The numbers live in a policy file judged by main's version and failing closed when it is missing (Option 3, stage ②), so the operator tightens them without a kit change. One escaped mistake demotes the change kind to high risk at once. The miss rate is the primary test because the operator's criterion is that the human is needed only where harm could slip through; the precision floor stops a noisy lens from earning trust by flagging everything.
- 2026-10-23 **Q4 · A change kind = the repo area the diff touches** (operator chose (a), my recommendation). Areas are the path rules the kit already keeps for slicing a diff among the review lenses (the lens-slice policy file); a diff touching several areas is judged by its riskiest one. Chosen because every PR feeds an area's record, so the 20-landing window of Q3 fills fastest, and because a path cannot be mislabelled the way a ticket stamp can. Ticket-declared kinds (b) lost on mislabelling.
- 2026-10-23 **Deferred: area × tier as the change kind** (operator). A finer split, taken up as its own policy change once per-area counts are deep enough to divide; recorded as a later PRD, not in the first set.
- 2026-10-23 **Q5 · An escaped mistake demotes its area, opens a revert or fix PR, and notifies the operator** (operator chose (b), my recommendation). When a low-risk landing is later counted a mistake (Q2), its area drops to high risk (Q3), the agent opens a revert PR (a fix PR where the revert conflicts), which waits for the operator because the area is now high risk, and the operator gets one notice naming the mistake, the PR and the area. Other areas keep their lane. A lane-wide stop (c) lost because it punishes areas that earned their record; the stage ③ kill switch stays the operator's manual stop.
- 2026-10-23 **Q6 · The agent decides what stays inside the change; what others rely on goes to the operator** (operator chose (c), my recommendation). On an unspecified behavior item, the agent decides and records the decision in the PR's decision log (stage ④) when its effect stays inside the change: a name, the structure, internal error wording, a test's shape. An unspecified item that changes what a user, a consumer or another skill sees or relies on (an output format, a flag, an exit code, a rule an agent obeys) makes the PR high risk. This narrows Q1's last item from "any unspecified behavior item" to "an unspecified change someone outside the PR relies on", and delivers idea 1's "make decisions on some ambiguous part and let the human know" for the rest. (b) lost because an outward-facing mistake reaches others before a later fix could count it.
